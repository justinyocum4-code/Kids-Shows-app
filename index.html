Kids Shows

Tap a show. It plays right here in the app.

Paste your own m3u playlist link below, then tap Load playlist. The app sorts the channels into sections.

Good to know

  • Every video plays inside this app. Nothing opens a web page or browser.
  • The 24/7 Live tab has only real, always-on live streams from official channels. If one ever says unavailable, pick another show.
  • The Playlist tab plays channels from any m3u playlist link you paste in. Those come from the playlist's own source, not from us.
  • You need an internet connection to stream.
  • Use your remote's arrow keys and OK button, or tap, to choose.
